# Albus by Diagon

> Find the equipment you need

Albus is Diagon's industrial equipment intelligence platform for buyers and sellers. Buyers can search across millions of industrial equipment listings, compare models, pricing, and availability, and connect with sellers faster, while sellers can publish AI-powered catalogs, reach qualified buyer demand, and see live equipment searches in real time.

## Main pages

- [Home](https://diagon.ai/): Buyer-facing homepage for industrial equipment search, comparison, and procurement.
- [Browse](https://diagon.ai/browse): Public browse surface for categories, OEMs, models, and used equipment listings.
- [Sell](https://diagon.ai/seller): Seller-facing overview of buyer demand, AI-powered catalogs, analytics, and bidding.

## Policies

- [Terms](https://diagon.ai/terms): Marketplace terms for buyers using Albus.
- [Privacy Policy](https://diagon.ai/privacy-policy): Privacy and security information for Albus.
- [Seller Terms](https://diagon.ai/seller-terms): Marketplace terms and conditions for sellers on Albus.
